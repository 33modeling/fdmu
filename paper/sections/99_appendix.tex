\section{Reproducibility and Statistical Details}
\label{app:evaluation-contract}
\label{app:datasets}
\label{app:objectives}

The released campaign manifest is the source of truth for every request,
candidate, semantic group, checkpoint, seed, random pool, and configuration
hash. Table~\ref{tab:datasets} is a readable digest, not a replacement for
that manifest. The denominator is the predeclared matrix: planned rows remain
visible when they are never attempted, terminate early, fail integrity, do not
reach the common forgetting criterion, or lack complete all-arm support.

The three target-request-disjoint development folds have distinct roles:
$\mathcal D_{\mathrm{cal}}$ fixes $(R,\eta,q_{\mathrm{fid}})$. If the realized
common fidelity support has size $N_{\mathrm{fid}}$, then
$K_{\mathrm{fid}}=\lceil q_{\mathrm{fid}}N_{\mathrm{fid}}\rceil$.
$\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}$ fixes $\widehat\alpha_{\mathrm{pred}}$, and
$\mathcal D_{\mathrm{prot}}$ fixes $K_p$,
$B_{\mathrm{tok}}$, and the common repair protocol.
The prediction rule maximizes equal-request Spearman correlation, breaking ties
by tail recall, then proximity to the midpoint, and finally the smaller
$\alpha$. Unresolved fallback weights are claim-ineligible.

The primary objective roster is GradDiff, NPO, SimNPO, GRU, RMU, RepNoise,
and Circuit Breakers; GA and IdkDPO are stress controls. For each parent the
artifact records the implementation identifier, source commit, trainable
block, learning rate, update and processed-token budgets, saved-step grid,
forget/reference coefficients, stopping rule, and hashes of all frozen
inputs. Readout labels are descriptive and never select a signal endpoint.

The candidate manifest freezes $\mathcal C$, the discovery/audit split by
semantic group, repair eligibility, and a score-independent random stream. Direct
answer duplicates, forget-fact paraphrases, and template-derived restatements
are ineligible for repair; entity overlap is retained as a disclosed stratum.

examples. Audit scores are computed at $\theta_0$ and sealed. Correlations use
midranks for tied values. Candidate IDs provide the predeclared deterministic
tie-break at every finite Top-$K$ boundary, including $K_{\mathrm{tail}}$,
$K_{\mathrm{fid}}$, and $K_p$.
The neutral stream and differentiable guard sets are selector-independent.
General-utility checkpoint selection uses a separate frozen set; neither
$\mathcal C_{\mathrm{audit}}$ nor its native retained outcome is used by a guard,
stopping rule, or checkpoint selector.

\subsection{Units, funnels, and decisions}
\label{app:outcomes}

Every prediction correlation is formed within one
model--request--seed--parent cell. Seeds are averaged within requests and
requests receive equal weight. The paired hierarchical bootstrap resamples
requests, trajectory seeds within requests, and semantic groups within
requests. Its replicate count, seed, and
finite-sample percentile rule are manifest fields. No candidate is pooled
across requests before a correlation is computed.

Within a request, empirical $\mathrm{CVaR}_{.95}$ is the mean of the largest
5\% damage values, using fractional weight at the quantile boundary. When an
audit has fewer than 20 candidates, this reduces to the maximum and the row is
flagged as low-tail-support rather than pooled with larger audits.

The audit exposes four denominators. $N_{\mathrm{prof}}$ counts all predeclared
profiles and $N_{\mathrm{valid}}$ those passing the outcome-blind integrity check.
$N_{\mathrm{att}}$ counts all predeclared parent trajectories and $N_{\mathrm{reach}}$
those reaching the common entry criterion. $N_{\mathrm{rv}}$ is the
reached--valid intersection. Prediction common support requires
$S_{\widehat\alpha_{\mathrm{pred}}}$,
both endpoints, the frozen simple control, and damage on the same units. A
tail cell is eligible only when its positive-damage count is at least
$K_{\mathrm{tail}}=\lceil q_{\mathrm{tail}}
|\mathcal C_{\mathrm{audit}}|\rceil$. Tail-ineligible cells remain in
the RQ1 correlation denominator; $N_{\mathrm{tail}}/N_{\mathrm{rv}}$ is always reported,
and the tail claim requires at least 0.80 coverage. Protection feasibility
requires $S_{\widehat\alpha_{\mathrm{pred}}}$, no repair, every frozen random
draw, $S_0$, and $S_1$ to
satisfy all four constraints on the same units; common support additionally
requires both damage outcomes and the native retained metric for every arm.
No missing outcome is imputed.

RQ1 is an intersection--union test over five prospective quantities: positive
rank association, positive paired gains over both signal endpoints and the
frozen simple control, and positive harmful-tail lift. The tail term
additionally requires at least 0.80 coverage. RQ2 is reserved for numerical
estimator validity on the declared setting-level fidelity support: bootstrap
lower bounds over that fidelity support must establish $f_\rho>0.80$ and
$f_K>0.70$. Each target profile separately requires perturbation survival,
split-bank rank agreement, and complete integrity coverage; these checks do
not exact-rank-certify its candidate support. The split-bank diagnostic is
not a confidence bound over unseen probe banks. RQ3 is the
intersection--union test over four comparators, two
$\Delta$NLL outcomes, and one native-metric non-inferiority margin per
comparator. Statistical success and eligibility are separate fields. A
fallback prediction weight, invalid profile, missing
expected cell, non-reach, infeasible arm, incomplete random-draw roster, or
incomplete common support forces claim pass to ``no,'' even if a conditional
effect is printable. For native metric $M_{\mathrm{nat}}$, a predeclared sign
$s_{\mathrm{nat}}\in\{-1,1\}$ orients larger values as better, and the
non-inferiority point estimate against comparator $a$ is
$\widehat h_a=s_{\mathrm{nat}}(M_{\mathrm{nat}}^{\mathrm{ours}}-
M_{\mathrm{nat}}^a)+\delta_{\mathrm{nat}}$. Let $L^{95}_a$ be its one-sided
95\% lower confidence bound; every comparator-specific $L^{95}_a$ must be
positive. The $q_G^\star$-substituted mixture selector is a
same-estimand numerical reference outside the protection IUT.

Parent rows are reported individually, and readout labels never select a signal.
Within each setting, full-chain support requires the setting's single
registered setting-level RQ2 certificate and every claim-bearing target profile
to pass its separate split-bank, perturbation-survival, and integrity checks,
plus at least one output-readout and one representation-readout parent to pass
prediction and protection under the frozen within-family Bonferroni rule.
Paper-level breadth additionally requires
the primary Qwen2.5-7B setting, at least one of the two model replications, and
at least two of the three dataset replications. Table~\ref{tab:robustness}
reports these denominators without pooling parents into a universal effect.

\section{Additional Prediction Analyses}
\label{app:prediction-supp}

The main paper defines the tail-prediction metric; Table~\ref{tab:tail-structure}
adds two outcome-only diagnostics. For the
$K_{\mathrm{tail}}=\lceil q_{\mathrm{tail}}N\rceil$ largest-damage candidates
$T_{q_{\mathrm{tail}}}$, define
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
q_{\mathrm{tail}}^{\mathrm{eff}}&=K_{\mathrm{tail}}/N,\\
M_{q_{\mathrm{tail}}}&=
\frac{\sum_{x\in T_{q_{\mathrm{tail}}}}[d_{t^\dagger}(x)]_+}
{\sum_x[d_{t^\dagger}(x)]_+}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
The reported
$M_{q_{\mathrm{tail}}}/q_{\mathrm{tail}}^{\mathrm{eff}}$ is positive
damage mass relative to a diffuse reference. Let $r(A)$ be the fraction of
candidate pairs in set $A$ that share a semantic group; the semantic-group
lift is $L_{\mathrm{grp}}=r(T_{q_{\mathrm{tail}}})/
r(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{audit}})$. Its
permutation test preserves request-specific group sizes and is reported only
when $K_{\mathrm{tail}}\geq 2$. A zero positive-damage
mass makes only $M_{q_{\mathrm{tail}}}/q_{\mathrm{tail}}^{\mathrm{eff}}$
undefined and never removes the cell from the prediction
denominator.

\section{Numerical Validation of Loss Susceptibility Estimation}
\label{app:estimator-stability}
\label{app:probe-details}
\label{app:randomized-impl}

For each Gaussian draw, the implementation normalizes $g_r$ to the unit
direction $v_r$, saves the declared parameter block, applies
$\theta_0+\eta v_r$ and $\theta_0-\eta v_r$ from that same copy, and restores
it before the next direction. Candidate order,
batching, masks, padding, precision, and dropout state are identical within a
pair. Signed responses are accumulated in fp32 and persisted before squaring,
so nested-$R$, direction-bank, and split-bank analyses require no new model
evaluation. The dimension factor in Eq.~\eqref{eq:loss-susceptibility-estimator} is
stored even though it does not affect ranking.

The confirmatory contract uses fp32 parameters. Lower-precision perturbation is
outside this contract because individual coordinate changes can fall below the
parameter format's resolution.

For the frozen diagnostic direction, let $\Delta\theta_{\mathcal B}$ be the
parameter displacement actually realized in the declared dtype after applying
$+\eta v$. Perturbation survival is
$\min\{\|\Delta\theta_{\mathcal B}\|_2/\eta,
d_{\mathcal B}^{-1}\sum_i\mathbf 1[\Delta\theta_i\ne0]\}$.
Split-bank rank agreement is the Spearman correlation between Loss
Susceptibility estimates formed from the two disjoint halves of the frozen
direction bank.

On the declared setting-level fidelity support, the direct-gradient reference
streams one candidate at a time, records its exact Loss Susceptibility, and
discards the gradient vector. Outcome-blind development
calibration freezes $(R,\eta)$ under the predeclared rule using exact-rank
fidelity, top-$K_{\mathrm{fid}}$ overlap, perturbation survival, and split-bank
rank agreement on that support. A target two-signal profile is valid only when
the perturbation-survival, split-bank-agreement, and integrity checks pass on
$\mathcal C_{\mathrm{disc}}$ before the sealed audit is opened; no
direct-gradient certificate is claimed for that target support.

\subsection{Common repair harness}
\label{app:guard-proof}
\label{app:repair-protocol}

Every confirmatory repair arm begins from the same first criterion-reaching
parent checkpoint and selects exactly $K_p$ eligible discovery examples. These
arms share the neutral stream $\mathcal R_0$, example order, optimizer random
stream, repair compute budget, constraint-gradient filter, guards, and save
schedule. The no-repair arm is the untouched entry-checkpoint comparator.
The neutral stream enters $L_{\mathrm{rep}}$ through the entry-anchored KL term in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:budgeted-repair}; the sealed two-signal score does not select it.
The token
budget charges protected, neutral, constraint-refresh, guard, and
checkpoint-evaluation tokens.

The damped constraint-gradient filter covers only differentiable surrogate
constraints. Each is oriented as $c_j(\theta)\leq0$ on a frozen guard set and
is active when $c_j(\theta)\geq-\kappa_j$, where $\kappa_j\geq0$. With
$\mathcal A=\{j:c_j(\theta)\geq-\kappa_j\}$ and
$G=[\nabla c_j(\theta)]_{j\in\mathcal A}$, the filtered repair gradient is
\begin{equation}
  \bar g_{\mathrm{rep}}
  =
  \left[I-G(G^\top G+\lambda I)^{-1}G^\top\right]
  \nabla L_{\mathrm{rep}}(\theta),
  \qquad \lambda>0.
  \label{eq:repair-filter}
\end{equation}
The matrix $G$ is refreshed every $m_{\mathrm{ref}}$ accepted steps. The ridge term
makes this a damped filter rather than an exact orthogonal projection.
For a forget token $(i,s)$, the anchored adverse margin is
\[
 r^{f}_{is}(\theta)=\log p_\theta(y_{is}\mid y_{i,<s})-
 \log p_{\theta_{t^\dagger}}(y_{is}\mid y_{i,<s});
\]
for a neutral or utility token it is the NLL increase
\[
 r^{u}_{is}(\theta)=-\log p_\theta(y_{is}\mid y_{i,<s})+
 \log p_{\theta_{t^\dagger}}(y_{is}\mid y_{i,<s}).
\]
A proposed step must keep every token margin below its frozen
$\epsilon^{\mathrm{tok}}$ and every example-average margin below
$\epsilon^{\mathrm{ex}}$. Otherwise the parameters are restored, the step size is
halved, and the step is retried up to $J_{\mathrm{retry}}$ times; exhaustion stops
the arm. The manifest freezes $\beta$, $\kappa_j$, $m_{\mathrm{ref}}$, $\lambda$,
both tolerances, $J_{\mathrm{retry}}$, and guard IDs before target runs.

Non-differentiable extraction, generation, and full utility metrics are not
represented in $G$. They are evaluated only at the shared save schedule on
selector-safe sets. The reported output is the last checkpoint satisfying
every row of Table~\ref{tab:constraint-contract}; if none does, the arm is
infeasible. Sealed $\Delta$NLL and native retained outcomes are opened only
after this selection. The artifact records refreshes, retries, accepted and
rejected updates, margins, and the selected step.

Repeated random allocation uses $B_{\mathrm{rand}}$ pools frozen before outcomes.
Every draw must complete and satisfy the same constraints. Draw IDs are
resampled and averaged inside their trajectory within each bootstrap
replicate. Diagnostic arms replace only the selector: the
$q_G^\star$-substituted mixture selector replaces $\widetilde q_G$ with the
separately discovery-rank-normalized exact coordinate
$\widetilde q_G^\star$ at the same frozen $\widehat\alpha_{\mathrm{pred}}$;
uniform rehearsal ignores ranking; and a realized-discovery-damage oracle
gives an unattainable repair-capacity reference. All remain outside the confirmatory comparison.

\subsection{Budget and specificity checks}
\label{app:specificity}

The selection-quota sweep varies the selection fraction $p$ and sets
$K_p=\lceil p|\mathcal C_{\mathrm{disc}}|\rceil$ while holding the repair compute
budget $B_{\mathrm{tok}}$ fixed. The specificity table reports
$\rho_G=\rho(S_0,d)$, $\rho_H=\rho(S_1,d)$, and
$\rho_S=\rho(S_{\widehat\alpha_{\mathrm{pred}}},d)$ for the rank-normalized
Loss Susceptibility endpoint, Representation Proximity endpoint,
and sealed two-signal score, respectively.

The specificity analysis keeps the sealed target two-signal profile fixed.
Other-request motion uses a size-matched forget set; random motion matches the target block
displacement and uses directions independent of profiling. These controls
bound interpretation: Loss Susceptibility conditions on
$\theta_0$ and $\mathcal B$ but not on the target request or direction of the
later unlearning update, whereas Representation Proximity contains the
request-conditioned information.
MUSE-Books and PISTOL are reported as boundary settings rather than primary
breadth evidence.

Hyperparameter grids, source-to-target weight carryover, exploratory traces of
directional persistence, and early hypothesis development are artifact
diagnostics rather than paper tables. They never select a target rule or fill
a confirmatory cell.
