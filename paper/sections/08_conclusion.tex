\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced a sealed, candidate-level risk profile for approximate LLM
unlearning. We estimate Loss Susceptibility---the squared norm of the loss
gradient in a declared parameter block---with pool-shared forward
perturbations, while
Representation Proximity adds
request-specific exposure. One development-calibrated score is fixed before
the target run and then evaluated in three distinct ways: prospective damage
prediction, numerical estimator fidelity on declared setting-level support,
and fixed-budget repair allocation.
\ConclusionHeadline\ By keeping the parent unlearner unchanged and testing protection directly on
an untouched audit fold, the framework connects early risk localization to
measurable decision value without treating correlation as a preservation
guarantee.
